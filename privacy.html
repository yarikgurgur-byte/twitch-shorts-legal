Twitch Shorts Pipeline

Privacy Policy

Effective: August 19, 2026

1. Scope

This policy describes how Twitch Shorts Pipeline handles information when an authorized operator connects a TikTok account and publishes video content.

2. Information processed

The application may process TikTok OAuth access and refresh tokens, the authorized user's open identifier and basic profile information, publication identifiers and statuses, captions, and video files selected by the operator for publication.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the authorized account, display the connected account, upload operator-approved videos, check publication results, prevent duplicate actions, diagnose failures, and maintain an audit record of requested publications.

4. Storage and retention

Credentials and operational records are stored on the operator-controlled computer and are not committed to a public source repository. Source media is retained when processing fails. Local media may be deleted after confirmed publication or an explicit rejection. Copies sent to Telegram or published to TikTok are retained according to those platforms' policies.

5. Sharing

Information is transmitted to TikTok only as required to authorize the account, upload content, and retrieve publication status. Selected media may also be sent to the operator's authorized Telegram chat for review. Personal information is not sold.

6. Security

The application uses access controls, local secret storage, authorized chat routing, and encrypted network connections where supported. No security method can guarantee absolute protection.

7. User choices and deletion

The authorized user may revoke TikTok access through TikTok settings and may request deletion of locally stored credentials and operational records. Content already published to TikTok must be managed through the connected TikTok account.

8. Children's privacy

The application is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when application functionality or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

10. Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests may be submitted through the public operator profile at github.com/yarikgurgur-byte.
